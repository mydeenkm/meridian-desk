# Damaged & Incorrect Shipments Policy

**Document ID:** POL-DMG-01 | **Version:** 1.2 | **Effective date:** 2026-02-01 | **Owner:** Logistics

## 1. Reporting window
Report damaged, missing or incorrect items within **5 business days of delivery**, with the order number, SKU, quantity and photos of the item and packaging.

## 2. Resolution
- Confirmed MS error or carrier damage: replacement or full credit, and MS pays return freight. No restocking fee.
- Claims reported after 5 business days are reviewed case by case and are not guaranteed.

## 3. Receiving tip
Note visible damage on the carrier's delivery receipt at the dock; it speeds up the carrier claim.
