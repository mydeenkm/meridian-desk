# Backorder & Stock Policy

**Document ID:** POL-BKO-01 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 2026-01-15 | **Owner:** Supply Chain

## 1. Out-of-stock items
Items with zero stock can be ordered and ship in **10 to 15 business days** after the order date.

## 2. Partial quantities
If stock covers only part of an order line, MS ships the available quantity and backorders the rest, unless the customer asks for a single shipment.

## 3. Stock figures
Stock levels in the catalog are updated daily and are not a reservation until the order is confirmed.
