# Invoice & Billing Disputes Policy

**Document ID:** POL-BIL-01 | **Version:** 1.5 | **Effective date:** 2026-01-15 | **Owner:** Finance

## 1. Dispute window
Disputes must be raised within **30 days of the invoice date**.

## 2. Pricing basis
Invoices use the catalog unit price in effect on the order date, less any bulk discount agreed in writing. Verbal or undocumented quotes are not honored; a written quote reference is required.

## 3. Resolution

| Finding | Action |
|---|---|
| Customer overcharged | Credit memo for the difference |
| Customer undercharged | Difference may be re-invoiced within 60 days |
| No error found | Dispute closed with explanation |

## 4. Payment during dispute
Undisputed amounts remain due by the original due date.
