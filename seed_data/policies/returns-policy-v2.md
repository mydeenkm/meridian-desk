# Returns & Restocking Policy

**Document ID:** POL-RET-01 | **Version:** 2.0 | **Effective date:** 2026-10-01 | **Owner:** Customer Operations

## 1. Return window
Customers may return eligible items within **21 days of delivery**. Requests received after day 21 are not accepted.

## 2. Restocking fee
All approved returns are charged a **20% restocking fee**, calculated on the returned line value at the billed unit price. The fee is deducted from the credit issued.

## 3. Eligibility

| Item type | Returnable? | Condition |
|---|---|---|
| Standard catalog items | Yes | Unused, original packaging |
| PPE and Abrasives | Yes | Sealed and unopened only |
| Hazardous goods (see POL-HAZ-01) | No | Not returnable under any circumstance |
| Special / custom orders (see POL-SPC-01) | No | Not returnable |

## 4. Process
1. Contact support with the order number, SKU, quantity and reason.
2. Support issues an RMA number. Returns without an RMA are refused at the dock.
3. The customer pays return freight unless the return is due to an MS error.
4. Credit is issued within 10 business days of the goods being received and inspected.

## 5. Applicability
This version applies to return requests received on or after its effective date.
