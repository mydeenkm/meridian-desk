# Credit Terms Policy

**Document ID:** POL-CRD-01 | **Version:** 2.0 | **Effective date:** 2026-01-01 | **Owner:** Finance

## 1. Credit tiers

| Tier | Account age | Maximum credit limit | Payment terms |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Under 12 months | $25,000 | Net 30 |
| B | 12 to 24 months | $75,000 | Net 30 |
| C | Over 24 months | $150,000 | Net 45 |

Net 60 and longer terms are not offered.

## 2. Applications and increases
Submit a credit application to Finance. Reviews take **5 to 7 business days**. Increases require two years of on-time payment history for Tier C.

## 3. Late payment
Invoices unpaid after the due date accrue a **1.5% monthly late fee**. Accounts more than 15 days past due are placed on credit hold, and open orders may be cancelled.

## 4. Early payment
A 2% discount applies if paid within 10 days of invoice date (not combinable with bulk discounts).
