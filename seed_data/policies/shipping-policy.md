# Shipping & Freight Policy

**Document ID:** POL-SHP-01 | **Version:** 3.0 | **Effective date:** 2026-02-01 | **Owner:** Logistics

## 1. Processing time
In-stock orders ship within **2 business days** of order placement. Hazardous goods add 3 business days (see POL-HAZ-01).

## 2. Carriers and transit
MS ships with FedEx Freight, UPS Supply Chain, XPO Logistics, Old Dominion and Estes Express. Standard ground transit within the contiguous US is **3 to 7 business days** from the ship date. Expedited (2 business days) is available for a 25% freight surcharge.

## 3. Freight charges (standard ground)

| Order value (before tax) | Freight charge |
|---|---|
| Under $1,000 | $65 flat |
| $1,000 to $4,999.99 | $120 flat |
| $5,000 and over | Free |

## 4. Delivery requirements
Liftgate and inside delivery must be requested before shipment and cost $45 each. Residential delivery is not offered.

## 5. Late shipments
An order is considered late only when it has not arrived after 7 business days from the ship date (ground). Freight charges are refunded only for late shipments or MS errors.
