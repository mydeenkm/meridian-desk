# Customer Support & Escalation Policy

**Document ID:** POL-SUP-01 | **Version:** 1.1 | **Effective date:** 2026-02-01 | **Owner:** Customer Operations

## 1. Hours and response times
Support is open Monday to Friday, 7:00 to 18:00 Central Time. Emails receive a first response within **1 business day**.

## 2. Escalation

| Situation | Escalate to |
|---|---|
| Disputed amount over $5,000 | Finance Manager |
| Damage or safety complaint | Quality Manager |
| Repeated delivery failures | Logistics Manager |
| Customer threatens to leave | Account Manager |

## 3. Identifying the customer
Always ask for the order number. If the customer cannot supply one, identify the order from the company name, products and approximate date.
