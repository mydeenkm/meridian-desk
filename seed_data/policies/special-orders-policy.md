# Special & Custom Orders Policy

**Document ID:** POL-SPC-01 | **Version:** 1.0 | **Effective date:** 2026-01-15 | **Owner:** Sales

## 1. Definition
A special order is any item not in the standard catalog or modified to customer specification.

## 2. Terms
- A **50% deposit** is required at order.
- Lead time is quoted per order, typically 4 to 8 weeks.
- Special orders are **non-cancellable** after deposit and **non-returnable**.
