# Order Cancellation Policy

**Document ID:** POL-CAN-01 | **Version:** 1.1 | **Effective date:** 2026-02-15 | **Owner:** Customer Operations

## 1. Customer cancellations

| Order status | Can the customer cancel? | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Processing, within 24 hours of order | Yes | None |
| Processing, after 24 hours | Yes, if not yet picked | 10% of order value |
| Shipped | No | Use the Returns Policy after delivery |
| Delivered | No | Use the Returns Policy |

## 2. MS-initiated cancellations
MS may cancel an order for credit hold (see POL-CRD-01), pricing errors, or discontinued stock. The customer is notified by email.

## 3. How to cancel
Email support with the order number. Cancellations are confirmed in writing.
