# Bulk Pricing Policy

**Document ID:** POL-BLK-01 | **Version:** 1.2 | **Effective date:** 2026-04-01 | **Owner:** Sales

## 1. Quantity discounts
Discounts apply to the catalog unit price and are based on the quantity of a **single SKU on a single order**. Quantities of different SKUs are not combined.

| Quantity of one SKU | Discount |
|---|---|
| 1 to 9 | 0% |
| 10 to 24 | 3% |
| 25 to 99 | 6% |
| 100 to 249 | 10% |
| 250 and over | Custom quote required |

## 2. Rules
- Discounts must be requested on the order or quote; they are not applied automatically.
- Bulk discounts cannot be combined with early-payment discounts.
- Quantities above current stock are subject to the Backorder Policy (POL-BKO-01).
- Quotes are valid for 15 days.
