# Product Warranty Policy

**Document ID:** POL-WAR-01 | **Version:** 2.1 | **Effective date:** 2026-01-15 | **Owner:** Quality & Compliance

## 1. Coverage
MS warrants that catalog products are free from manufacturing defects for the period shown below, counted from the delivery date.

| Category | Warranty period | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Valves & Fittings | 24 months | Excludes damage from incorrect media or pressure |
| Electrical | 24 months | Must be installed by qualified personnel |
| Hydraulics | 18 months | Seals and filter elements are consumables, 90 days |
| Fasteners | 12 months | Manufacturing defects only |
| Power & Hand Tools | 12 months | Excludes wear items such as blades and brushes |
| Safety & PPE | 90 days | Defects only, not wear or misuse |
| Abrasives | None | Consumable |
| Facility & Janitorial | 90 days | Chemicals: shelf-life defects only |

## 2. Making a claim
- Report the defect within **30 days of discovering it**, with the order number, SKU, quantity and photos.
- Defective items are replaced or credited at MS's choice. Warranty claims are not "returns" and carry no restocking fee.
- MS may request that failed items be sent back for inspection.

## 3. Exclusions
Misuse, unauthorized modification, normal wear, and damage in transit (handled under POL-DMG-01).
